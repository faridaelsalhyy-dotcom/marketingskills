# Points of Measure (POM) Library

Standard points of measure by garment type. All measurements taken with the garment laid flat, buttoned/zipped, without stretching, unless noted. **State whether widths are ½ (flat, edge to edge) or full circumference.**

## General Conventions

- **HPS** — high point shoulder, where the shoulder seam meets the neckline
- **CF / CB** — center front / center back
- **Armhole** — measured straight or curved; state which
- **Relaxed vs extended** — for elastic or stretch areas, give both (e.g., waist relaxed and waist extended)
- Tolerances below are common starting points; adjust to the brand's quality standard and fabric (knits need looser tolerances than wovens)

## Grading Starting Points (alpha womenswear, cm)
