# Trend Signal Sources

Where trend evidence comes from, what each source is good for, and how it misleads. A trend is credible when it shows up in **three or more independent source types**.

## Source Map

| Source type | Examples | Lead time | Best for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cultural drivers** | Economic data, tech launches, film/TV, music, art shows, sport, gaming, politics | 2–10 years | Macro direction, mood, the "why" | Over-interpreting single events |
| **Runway** | Paris, Milan, London, New York; Copenhagen, Seoul, Tokyo, Lagos, São Paulo; graduate shows | 6–24 months | Silhouette, color, material innovation | Runway is theatre; commercial versions are diluted |
| **Independent designers** | Emerging labels, Kickstarter/indie shops, craft communities | 1–3 years | Early introduction signals | Tiny sample sizes |
| **Street style & subculture** | Fashion-week street style, club/festival scenes, local neighborhoods | 6–18 months | How people actually combine and wear things | Street style at fashion weeks is often gifted/staged |
| **Social platforms** | TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube, Xiaohongshu (RED), Douyin | 0–12 months | Micro trends, styling, velocity | Algorithmic amplification ≠ purchase intent; bot/brand-seeded content |
| **Search** | Google Trends, Pinterest Trends / Pinterest Predicts, marketplace search bars | 0–12 months | Intent and momentum | Seasonality; news spikes |
| **Resale** | Depop, Vinted, Vestiaire Collective, The RealReal, eBay, Grailed | 6–24 months | Revival signals, archive demand | Collector niches skew data |
| **Retail tracking** | New-in counts, sell-outs, restocks, markdown depth across retailers; tools like Edited, Trendalytics | 0–6 months | Stage on lifecycle (rise vs peak) | Lagging indicator for leaders |
| **Forecasting services** | WGSN, Heuritech, Trendstop, Fashion Snoops, Stylus, Coloro | 12–24 months | Consolidated direction, color systems | Everyone subscribes, so it homogenizes |
| **Trade fairs** | Première Vision (fabrics), Pitti Uomo (menswear), Lineapelle (leather), Texworld, Munich Fabric Start | 12–18 months | Material and finish innovation | Supplier-led, not consumer-led |
| **Color authorities** | Pantone, Coloro, Color Marketing Group | 18–24 months | Palette direction | Color of the Year is partly marketing |
| **Own data** | Sell-through by attribute, returns reasons, customer service notes, site search | Real time | Customer-specific truth | Only shows what you already stocked |

## Reading Momentum

Measure the **slope**, not the level:

- **Rising** — 3+ consecutive periods of growth, growing creator diversity, retailers adding SKUs, low markdowns
- **Peaking** — growth slows, dupes and mass retail everywhere, first markdowns appear, "how to style X" content peaks
- **Declining** — search and social falling, markdown depth increasing, "is X over?" discourse, resale prices falling

## Evidence Log Format

Record each signal so the forecast can be audited later:

| Date | Source type | Source | Observation | Theme | Strength (1–3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-10 | Runway | Paris SS27, 4 houses | Dropped waist dresses in sheer layers | Soft Romance | 3 |
| 2026-09-14 | Search | Google Trends, "drop waist dress" | +60% YoY, 3 months rising | Soft Romance | 2 |

## Cultural Driver Prompts

Ask these every season when scanning for macro drivers:

1. What is the economic mood — confident, anxious, or recovering? (Anxiety tends to push toward investment pieces, nostalgia, and comfort.)
2. What new technology changes how people live, shop, or see themselves?
3. What values are rising — sustainability, craft, inclusivity, heritage, wellness?
4. Which films, series, artists, or athletes dominate attention with a strong visual identity?
5. Which generation is entering peak spending, and what did their parents wear at their age?
6. What climate realities shift seasonality (warmer autumns, transitional layering, rain gear)?
