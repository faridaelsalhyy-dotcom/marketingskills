# Comp Shop Template

A structured competitor-shopping template. Complete one sheet per competitor, then consolidate into the summary matrix.

## Before You Shop

- Define the **category scope** (e.g., "women's dresses and knit tops") — comp shopping everything produces noise
- Fix the **date and channel** (online and/or store, which city) — assortments change weekly
- Use the same **size and color** where possible when comparing quality (e.g., size M, black)
- Photograph (where permitted) or screenshot product, labels, and price tags

### Assortment Mix

- Basics / core vs fashion / seasonal share
- Color split: neutrals / accents / brights
- Newness cadence: how many new styles per week or month
- Key silhouettes and details this season

### Brand Experience

- Site/store experience, photography style, model diversity
- Packaging and unboxing
- Social presence: platforms, follower size, content type, creator partnerships
- Community or loyalty program

### Strengths / Weaknesses / Opportunity for Us

- 3 things they do better than us
- 3 gaps or complaints (from reviews, fit, quality, service)
- What we can credibly own against them

## Positioning Map Instructions

1. Choose two axes customers use to choose (validate with interviews)
2. Score each competitor 1–10 on each axis with evidence from the comp shop
3. Plot; mark our current and target position
4. Label white space and state the evidence that customers want it

## Review Mining Prompts

Scan 50–200 reviews per key competitor product and tag:

- **Fit** — runs small/large, length, specific body areas
- **Fabric** — thin, pilling, see-through, shrinkage, itchy
- **Construction** — seams, zips, buttons, durability
- **Color** — differs from photos
- **Value** — worth the price or not
- **Occasion** — where people wear it

The most frequent complaints about competitors are the product brief for your differentiation.
