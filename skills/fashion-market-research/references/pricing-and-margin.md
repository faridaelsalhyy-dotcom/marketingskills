# Fashion Pricing & Margin Formulas

Formulas for costing, pricing, and evaluating a fashion assortment.

## Cost Build-Up (Landed Cost)

```
FOB / ex-factory cost   (fabric + trims + CMT/labor + factory margin)
+ Freight & insurance
+ Duty (varies by HS code, fiber content, and origin country)
+ Customs brokerage, fees, domestic transport
= LANDED COST (per unit)
```

**CMT** = cut, make, trim (labor). **FOB** = free on board (factory price at port). **DDP** = delivered duty paid (supplier covers delivery and duty).

Fiber composition changes duty rates — always confirm the HS code with a customs broker before finalizing cost.

## Pricing Chains

### DTC (direct to consumer)

```
Retail price = Landed cost × DTC multiplier (commonly ~3–5×)
Gross margin % = (Retail − Landed cost) ÷ Retail
```

Example: landed cost $25 × 4 = $100 retail → 75% gross margin (before marketing, fulfillment, returns, discounts).

### Wholesale

```
Wholesale price = Landed cost × ~2.0–2.5
Retail (MSRP)   = Wholesale × retailer markup (commonly ~2.0–2.8×, "keystone" = 2×)
```

Example: landed $25 → wholesale $55 → MSRP $135.

**Keep DTC and wholesale MSRP identical** to protect retail partners; plan margin accordingly.

## Contribution Margin (DTC reality check)

```
Retail price
− Discounts and markdowns (average realized price, not full price)
− Landed cost
− Payment processing
− Pick, pack, shipping
− Returns cost (return rate × (shipping + processing + unsellable %))
− Customer acquisition cost per order
= Contribution margin per order
```

Many DTC fashion brands look profitable at full price and lose money at realized price after returns and acquisition.

## Price Architecture (Good / Better / Best)

| Tier | Role | Share of options | Share of units |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Entry / good** | Accessible trial, traffic driver | ~25–35% | ~35–50% |
| **Core / better** | Volume and margin engine | ~40–50% | ~35–45% |
| **Hero / best** | Brand statement, halo, press | ~15–25% | ~10–20% |

Keep clear **price gaps** between tiers (commonly 20–40%) so the step-up is justified by visible value.

### Price Point Psychology

- Charm pricing ($79, $149) reads as value; round pricing ($80, $150) reads as premium
- Keep price endings consistent within a brand
- Anchor with a hero item — a $600 coat makes a $220 knit look accessible

## Target Costing (price-first)

Set the retail price from market research, then work backward:

```
Target landed cost = Target retail × (1 − target gross margin %)
```

Example: $120 target retail at 72% margin → target landed cost $33.60. Engineer fabric, trims, construction, and factory to hit it.

## Sell-Through & Open-to-Buy

```
Sell-through %  = Units sold ÷ Units received × 100
Weeks of supply = Current inventory ÷ Average weekly sales
Open-to-buy     = Planned sales + Planned end inventory + Planned markdowns − Beginning inventory − On order
GMROI           = Gross margin $ ÷ Average inventory at cost
```

Plan buy quantities from forecast sell-through, not optimism: an under-bought hero that sells out beats an over-bought one discounted by 50%.
